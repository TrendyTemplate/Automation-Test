How it works

A team of focused AI agents handles the repetitive parts. Your team stays in control of every posting.

1 · Find the gaps

Bank feed vs documents

Each morning DocChase compares every client's Xero bank transactions with the documents received and lists what's missing.

2 · Chase the client

Polite, persistent reminders

Clients get a clear list ("12 Oct, £450, Screwfix") by email or WhatsApp, with follow-ups on your schedule and escalation to you if they go quiet.

3 · Read & draft

Captured, coded, ready to approve

Receipts are read, matched, given a suggested account and VAT rate, checked for duplicates and odd bank details, then drafted into Xero.

Questions

Does it post anything to Xero automatically?

No. Everything goes in as a draft for your team to approve. Unusual items (duplicates, changed bank details, VAT mismatches) are flagged for review.

Is this connected to Xero officially?

DocChase connects through Xero's standard API using the permissions you grant. DocChase is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xero.

Where is client data stored, and who sees it?

During the pilot we agree a data processing agreement with you first. Documents are encrypted in storage and used only to deliver the service to your practice.

Is it live yet?

Not as self-serve software. We're running hands-on pilots with a small number of practices first, so the product is built around how real practices work.
