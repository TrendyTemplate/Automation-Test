Questions, answered

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook Messenger (your Page inbox), Instagram Direct (professional accounts linked to a Page) and the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Everything goes through Meta's official APIs — no unofficial WhatsApp hacks that get numbers banned.

Will the AI tell customers a wrong price?

No. The AI only sees prices from your product list, order totals are calculated by our server, and any reply mentioning an amount not in your catalog or knowledge base is held for your approval instead of being sent.

Can I check replies before they go out?

Yes. Every channel starts in Draft mode: the assistant writes, you approve with one click. Switch to Auto-pilot when you're comfortable — it still holds anything uncertain.

What happens when I reply myself?

The assistant steps back for that customer for 12 hours — whether you reply from ChatPilot or from Meta's own inbox — so it never talks over you.

Does it understand Bangla and Banglish?

Yes — it replies in the customer's own language and script, and understands mixed Banglish like “2ta L size nibo”.

Do I need Meta approval?

To answer the public, your Meta app needs App Review for messaging permissions. Until then, admins and testers of your app can try it. Our setup guide walks you through it step by step.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription in one click from the billing page, and export or delete all your data whenever you like.
