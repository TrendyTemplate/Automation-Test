> Check the App Store for apps with the same or a similar name before submitting. Keep claims factual; Shopify rejects listings with unverifiable claims, superlatives about competitors or guaranteed results.

## App name
Delaywise: Late Order Alerts

## Tagline (max ~62 characters)
Tell customers about late parcels before they ask

## Introduction
Stop “Where is my order?” emails before they start. Delaywise spots stalled, late and lost parcels and sends your customer a friendly update in their own language.

## Details
Late parcels create angry emails, bad reviews and chargebacks, usually because the customer heard nothing. Delaywise checks your shipped orders every hour. When a parcel stops moving, passes its expected date, hits a carrier problem or looks lost, Delaywise drafts a short, honest update to your customer, written in their language and in your brand's tone. Approve each email with one click, edit it, or switch on Autopilot. For parcels that are days late, add a single-use apology discount automatically. A carrier scorecard shows which carriers keep their promises, and the dashboard shows how much order value is at risk right now.

## Key features (3–5 bullets)
- Early warning for stalled parcels, before the delivery date passes
- Emails in your customer's language and your brand's tone, ready to approve or sent on Autopilot
- Optional single-use apology discount for late or lost orders
- Carrier scorecard: on-time rate and average delay per carrier
- Works with your existing tracking app; no theme changes, no storefront slowdown

## Testing instructions for the review team
1. Install the app on the review store. It opens on the Dashboard.
2. Create two test orders with a shipping address and fulfill both with a tracking number (carrier UPS).
3. In **Settings**, set “Usual delivery time” to 1 day and “Grace period” to 0 hours, then click **Save**.
4. On the **Dashboard**, click **Scan shipments now**. Orders past the expected date appear under “Shipments that need attention”.
5. Open **Messages**: each late order has a draft email. Edit the text and click **Send to customer**; the email goes to the order's email address.
6. **Settings → Preview an email** shows sample emails in other languages without sending anything.
7. Paid features (Autopilot, apology discounts) can be tested after choosing a plan on the **Plans** page (test charges on development stores).
