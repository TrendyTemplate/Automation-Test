Questions, answered

Which email providers are supported?

Any provider with IMAP and SMTP: Gmail & Google Workspace, Outlook & Microsoft 365, Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, Yandex, and custom-domain email from cPanel, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Namecheap and others. Gmail and Microsoft accounts can also connect with secure one-click sign-in.

Will the AI send something wrong to my customers?

You decide how much it does on its own. In Draft mode nothing is sent without your approval. On Autopilot, a reply only goes out when it's confident, grounded in your knowledge base, and passes every safety check — and even then you get an undo window. VIPs, refunds, legal topics and anything uncertain always wait for you.

Can someone trick the AI with a malicious email?

Emails that try to give the AI instructions are detected and held for review. The AI also has no ability to send email anywhere except back to the original sender, and every reply is checked for unknown links, third-party addresses and secrets before it can leave.

Who can read my emails?

Only you and your team. Content is encrypted at rest with a key unique to your workspace. Our support team can only look if you grant temporary access, and you can see every visit in your security log. We never sell data or use it to train AI models.

Does it work in my language?

Yes — it understands and replies in 40+ languages, matching the customer's language automatically, and can translate any email or preview a reply in your own language before you send it.

What does "Lifetime" include?

One payment, no subscription. You keep the Lifetime plan's accounts and features, including a monthly AI allowance.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription in one click from the billing page, and export or delete all your data whenever you like.
