Questions

Which email providers are supported?

Any provider that supports IMAP and SMTP: Gmail & Google Workspace, Outlook & Microsoft 365, Yahoo, iCloud, Zoho, Yandex, and custom-domain email from cPanel, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Namecheap and others.

Will the AI send something wrong to my customers?

You decide. In Draft mode nothing is sent without your approval. In Auto-pilot, the AI only sends when it's confident and the answer is grounded in your knowledge base; anything sensitive (refunds, legal, complaints it can't resolve) is held for a human.

Is my data safe?

Mailbox passwords are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. We recommend app passwords, which you can revoke at any time. We don't use your emails to train AI models.

What does "Lifetime" include?

One payment, no subscription. You keep the Lifetime plan's mailboxes and features forever, including a monthly AI reply allowance.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the Billing page with one click.
