How we protect your email

Your inbox holds your business. These are the safeguards built into MailPilot AI.

Encryption

  • Mailbox passwords, sign-in tokens and email content are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Every workspace has its own data key, which is itself encrypted by a master key kept outside the database. Each encrypted value is bound to its purpose, so it can't be moved or reused.
  • Deleting a workspace destroys its key, making any remaining data unreadable (crypto-shredding).
  • All connections use TLS 1.2+ with certificate verification — including connections to your mail servers.

Account protection

  • Two-factor authentication with any authenticator app; required for platform administrators.
  • Server-side sessions you can see and revoke; alerts on sign-ins from new devices; automatic sign-out of other devices after a password change.
  • Brute-force protection with progressive account lockout and rate limiting; passwords hashed with bcrypt.
  • Emergency lockdown: one click disconnects all mailboxes, revokes access, deletes API keys and signs out everyone.

Least privilege

  • Our staff can only access your account if you grant time-limited support access. Every visit is recorded in your security log.
  • When you remove a Google account, we revoke our access at Google immediately.
  • API keys only reach your data API — never billing, account or admin functions.

AI safety

  • Card numbers, IBANs, passwords and one-time codes are masked before any email reaches the AI (Privacy Shield).
  • The AI cannot choose recipients, call tools or reach the internet: replies always go back to the original sender only.
  • Emails that try to instruct the AI (prompt injection) are detected and held for a human.
  • Every automatic reply passes deterministic checks for unverified prices, unknown links, third-party addresses and secrets.
  • A transparency log shows every time the AI processed one of your emails. Your data is never used to train AI models.

Application security

  • Strict Content Security Policy with no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers; HSTS; clickjacking protection.
  • Cross-site request forgery protection on every state-changing request.
  • Outbound requests (webhooks, website import, unsubscribe) can never reach internal networks.
  • Signed webhooks (HMAC-SHA256) so your systems can verify every event.

Your control

  • Export all your data or delete your account at any time, self-service.
  • Configurable automatic deletion of email content after 30–365 days.
